Fixing the parser does not close #4471. The other two problems have their own causes and need their own fixes.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants on file write the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped (“7 April went out on 4 July”). This is the bug #4471 is named after, and the fix that is queued.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, then reads the value the tenant’s way, day first. People follow the hint, so the date flips there before the parser runs. It shows up only in reports that came in through the form at tenants on booking-v3. The body reads the same as a parser report, so subject and body alone cannot tell them apart; tenant and channel can. A parser fix will not touch it: the hint must match the locale, or the form must stop guessing.

one-day: the date slips by one day. It is not flipped. A meeting on Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon got Tuesday and one in Toronto got Monday; an invite arrived before the booking was made. That is a time-zone shift (a date stored or sent at midnight in one zone and read in another), not a format bug. The desk read these as one-day moves and footnoted them under parser; they belong in a ticket of their own.

How the reports were counted

  • The export has overlapping pages, so some reports come twice. One id, one report.
  • parser and hint come from causeOf in causes.ts: the tenant’s locale and booking form, not intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong whenever someone in a day-first business reports from an en-US browser.
  • one-day is the desk’s own list (desk-notes.json). Every other report names two dates that are the same day and month swapped, or says the dates are mixed up.

What to do with #4471

  • Keep #4471 for parser. Open its two siblings and move their reports there.
  • Do not mark #4471 known again until the counts on this page are split across three tickets.
  • Dedupe on subject merged three bugs into one ticket. When an incident’s reports describe different things in their bodies, split before you merge.
